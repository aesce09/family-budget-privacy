# family-budget-privacy# Privacy Policy for Family Budget: Group & Solo

**Effective Date:** November 13, 2026

Ashley parker ("we," "our," or "us") operates the **Family Budget: Group & Solo** mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect
We collect only the information necessary to provide shared and solo budgeting features:
- **Account Information:** Your email address and authentication credentials when you create an account.
- **Profile & Household Information:** Your chosen display name, profile ring color, household budget name, and household invite code.
- **Financial & Budgeting Data:** Spending categories, budget limits, expense amounts, transaction descriptions, and transaction timestamps that you manually enter into the App. We do **not** connect to your bank accounts, credit cards, or financial institutions.
- **Support Communications:** If you choose to contact us or submit a bug report via email, we receive your email address, message, and basic device/app version diagnostics included in the email.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use your information strictly to:
- Authenticate your account and keep you signed in.
- Sync budgets, categories, and expenses in real time across devices in your household.
- Calculate weekly and monthly budget summaries and spending analytics.
- Respond to support requests and fix reported bugs.

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to third parties, and we do **not** use third-party advertising trackers or cross-app tracking.

## 3. Data Storage & Third-Party Service Providers
Your account and budget data are securely stored and synced using **Supabase** (cloud database and authentication infrastructure) and cached locally on your device for offline reliability. Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.

## 4. Shared Household Visibility
If you create or join a shared household budget using an invite code, other members of that same household (up to 8 members) can view the shared categories, budget limits, expense entries, display names, and ring colors within that household.

## 5. Data Retention & Account Deletion
You can delete individual transactions or categories at any time inside the App. You may also permanently delete your account and remove your profile from the App at any time by going to **Settings → Account → Delete Account**. When you delete your account, your authentication record and member profile are permanently removed from our active systems.

## 6. Children's Privacy
The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
